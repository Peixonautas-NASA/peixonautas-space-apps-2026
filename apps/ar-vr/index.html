Settings

Hold Walk to move freely in the direction you are looking, including uphill. This pauses autopilot. Press Play to resume following the route. After arrival, Play restarts the route. The target button points you toward the route.

Maps: drag the title bar to move a panel; it stays at a fixed distance from you. On the 3D Map, drag the terrain model to rotate it. X closes the panel; the top-right selector reopens it in front of you. Maps follow you even when you leave the route.

Sensors: lower values mean smoother motion, with less jitter and more delay. When sensors are enabled, dragging adjusts heading only.

Desktop: Space pauses; W/A/S/D or arrow keys move freely; Shift moves 5x faster.

—

Terrain: Gale uses prepared HiRISE and HRSC/MOLA DEMs where available. Other regions use MOLA ClrShade chromaticity reconstruction (-8.2 to +21.2 km). The optional detail texture is procedural.
